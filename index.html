🌊 tide-games

games that share one sea

One secp256k1 key is a nostr identity, a Bitcoin address, and a signer — and your balance is a signed trail any game can verify, not a number in anyone's database. Every game here is pure static, every pixel from code, and where chance sails, the next Bitcoin block decides — nobody knows the outcome before the tide comes in, and anyone can check it after. The pattern is documented in BUILDING-GAMES.md — build a boat and join the fleet.
